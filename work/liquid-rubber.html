About the project

Liquid Rubber, a leader in waterproof coatings and sealants, was rebranded and digitally redesigned to support growth and improve customer engagement. The project focused on simplifying navigation, clarifying product information, and creating visually engaging content to better serve DIY users. A refreshed brand identity, intuitive website, and targeted campaigns helped build trust, enhance user experience, and position the brand for long-term success.
